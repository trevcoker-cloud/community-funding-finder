        How this list is checked

        Every programme in this index was checked against the funder’s own pages on 22–23 October 2026. Each entry records eligibility separately for CICs, registered charities and non-profit or community organisations, and links to the source page used.

        What the labels mean

        • Eligible — the funder names that legal form, or its published criteria clearly cover it.
        • Check — the form appears to fit, or the page could not be fully checked. Confirm before writing an application.
        • Not eligible — the funder excludes that form, or restricts applications to registered charities.

        Organisation types

        • CIC — a community interest company limited by guarantee or shares.
        • Charity — a registered charity or charitable incorporated organisation (CIO).
        • Non-profit — a constituted voluntary, community or not-for-profit organisation. Unincorporated groups should check whether the funder requires a particular legal form.

        Open and close dates

        • Rolling means the programme is open with no published closing date. It is still shown as open, but can pause at short notice.
        • Round-based funds have fixed windows; dates shown are for the current or next known round.
        • Closed / paused entries are kept so you can see status at a glance and prepare for the next round.

        What is not here

        This is a curated starting point, not a complete register. Local community foundations, lottery distributors in the devolved nations and corporate foundations run many more schemes. Funds that exclude every one of these organisation types are left out; funds that serve only charities, such as Garfield Weston and the Leathersellers, are included and labelled “Not eligible” for CICs. Closed and paused programmes are kept where they are likely to reopen, with the status shown on the card.

        Checking for changes

        Every time the page opens, it re-fetches all monitored funder pages and compares them with the last saved snapshots, then lists any new dates, amounts and round wording in the strip above. Detected changes stay listed for 30 days or until you mark them reviewed, so a reload cannot hide them. It reports for review and never edits the dataset automatically. A changed page is a prompt to read the source, not proof that a deadline moved.

        Always read the current guidance. Nothing here is legal or financial advice.
